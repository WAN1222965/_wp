The Art of Vintage Blogging

Posted on May 18, 2026 by Admin

Welcome to the classic WordPress experience. This layout is inspired by the Twenty Ten theme — the first default theme introduced with WordPress 3.0. It paired a traditional two-column layout with a clean, typography-driven design that set the standard for modern blogging.

Twenty Ten was designed by the WordPress team and released in June 2010. It features a header image, a top navigation bar, a sidebar with widget areas, and a footer with site information. The design emphasizes readability and simplicity.

Why Serif Headings Still Matter

Posted on May 15, 2026 by Staff

In an era of minimalist sans-serif everything, there's something refreshing about serif headlines. Georgia and Times New Roman bring warmth, authority, and a sense of permanence to long-form reading. The Twenty Ten theme understood this well.

Pairing Georgia headlines with Arial body text created a clear visual hierarchy — one that guided the reader's eye naturally from title to content. It wasn't just a design choice; it was a statement about what blogging should be: thoughtful, approachable, and human.
